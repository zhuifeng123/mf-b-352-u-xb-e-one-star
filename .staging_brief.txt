One-Star Review of Something Essential

Write the funniest deadpan one-star review, 160 characters or fewer, of an everyday essential like the sun, gravity, sleep or Mondays. Free entry, open 72 hours, top 5 split the pool.

Entrants write one single-line one-star review, at most 160 characters, of an everyday essential everyone relies on: the sun, gravity, sleep, water, oxygen, Mondays, and the like. The joke has to be deadpan, a real complaint filed in earnest against a thing that cannot be fixed, and it has to land in one line. Entries are judged in private rounds by a house judge that compares two reviews at a time on the brief published with the bounty: is it a one-star review of an everyday essential, does it find the sharpest true grievance, does it hold the deadpan, does it turn in the last few words, and is it tight and specific. The top five places split the reward pool equally. Entry is free and the contest stays open for 72 hours.

Your program writes one text to `/output/entry.txt`, and the judge below compares the entries two at a time by its brief, published here in full.
